# Design Studio imagery — provenance

Two kinds of image live in this directory.

## App screenshots (real)

Captured on 2026-11-14 from Aeros Design Studio running locally (the same
build as design-studio.aeros-x.com), at 1600 × 1000 CSS px and 2× scale, then
resized to 2400 px wide WebP. Every model, dimension, flat pattern, drawing and
print check on screen was produced by the app's own geometry kernel.

- `modeler.webp`: the Solid workspace with the sample mounting plate
- `sketch.webp`: a dimensioned sketch with its degrees-of-freedom read-out
- `surface-zebra.webp`: zebra-stripe analysis in the Surface workspace
- `sheet-metal.webp`: a base flange with one edge flange, bend allowance in the panel
- `assembly.webp`: two saved parts and one flush mate
- `carton-flat.webp` / `carton-folded.webp`: the tuck-end carton template with artwork (kraft flood, logo, text), flat and folded
- `cup-studio.webp`: the TPC 350 ml double-wall die with artwork applied
- `drawing.webp`: an A3 drawing generated from the mounting plate
- `assistant.webp` / `assistant-dark.webp`: the Assistant building a mounting plate as a proposal

The Assistant shots were taken with the API's scripted demo stand-in
(`AI_DEMO=1`), which drives the same tools and kernel as the live model. One
line of its reply was shortened to drop the words "Demo mode:".

## Renders (AI-generated)

- `render-cup.webp`: a plain black paper cup on paper
- `render-carton.webp`: a white carton blank beside the folded box

These are **AI-generated renders, not photographs**. They were made with
Higgsfield (`marketing_studio_image`) on 2026-11-14 and are used as mood
imagery only. They are unbranded and must not be captioned as output exported
from Design Studio or as work delivered to a client.
